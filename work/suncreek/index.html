Sun Creek
Farm Foods

  • Brand Identity
  • Package Design
  • Web Design
  • Web Development

When partnering with Sun Creek Farm Foods, our mission was to craft a brand identity as bold and inviting as the flavors they create. We developed an eye-catching design that captures the vibrancy and energy of their small-batch products.

Our design choices extended to dynamic packaging that combines bold typography with vivid, illustrative elements, giving each hot sauce flavor a unique personality while maintaining a cohesive brand feel. The eye-catching visuals were designed not just to draw attention but to tell the story of Sun Creek Farm Foods’ dedication to quality and bold flavours.
